StatCaddie foreground service demonstration

This clean-install Wear OS recording demonstrates StatCaddie's user-initiated golf-round foreground service.

  • The golfer explicitly taps Start round and selects a course.
  • Android requests precise location, physical activity, notification, and heart-rate access in context.
  • The active round screen provides live golf-distance and score controls.
  • The notification shade shows the ongoing Round in progress notification.
  • The golfer opens the scorecard and taps Finish round, which stops the service.

The recording uses a synthetic offline demo course and an Android 16 Wear OS emulator. It contains no personal data.
